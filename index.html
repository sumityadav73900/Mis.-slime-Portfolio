1. The Beginning

In a quiet, enchanted forest, pure drops of mana floated gently in the air. A young magic apprentice whispered a tiny spell to gather some of this magic for the very first time. Instead of just glowing, a stray drop of blue mana mixed with a fresh morning dewdrop on a flower petal. Suddenly, with a soft pop!, the pure energy turned into a squishy jelly. Two glowing, curious eyes opened up, and it bounced with a happy boing! Right there, born from pure mana and a little magic accident, was an adorable, palm-sized slime!. That was me!

🧙

2. The hat that fit

A tired old witch dropped her crystal hat from her broom. It landed on my head, glowed pink, and refused to leave. Neither did I.

🫚

3. The hat lessons

The hat taught me The slime can naturally melt objects down into raw mana. The hat teaches it control: "Don't melt everything you touch! Dissolve rotten logs, trash, or enemy weapons—not a friend's shoes!" Because the slime is so young and innocent, the hat acts as its worldly guide—warning it about dangerous monsters, pointing out sweet berries, and explaining why humans run away screaming whenever they see a slime.

🌙
